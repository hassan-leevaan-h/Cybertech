// Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do we need a lot of data to start?

No. Useful insight often comes from a few well-chosen sources. We start small, prove the value and expand from there.

Where will our data be stored?

In your own cloud account or on-premise systems wherever possible. We never move your data without written agreement.

Can you work with our existing tools?

Yes. We build on tools you already use, such as Power BI, Grafana or Splunk, and only recommend new ones when they are clearly needed.

Will our staff be able to maintain the dashboards?

That is the goal. Every project includes documentation and hands-on training for your team.
