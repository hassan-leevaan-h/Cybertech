What we do

Secure-by-design software, built to ship

// Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who owns the code?

You do. All source code and documentation transfer to you on final payment, and we never reuse client-specific code.

Can you take over an existing codebase?

Yes. We start with a two-week code and security audit, then agree a plan to stabilise, document and extend it.

How do you price projects?

Well-defined projects get a fixed price. Evolving products work better on a monthly team rate. We recommend whichever fits after discovery.

Do you support the software after launch?

Yes. Every project includes 30 days of support, and optional monthly plans cover monitoring, patching and new features.
