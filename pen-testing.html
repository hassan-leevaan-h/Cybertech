// Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Will testing disrupt our systems?

We agree test windows and rules of engagement in advance, avoid destructive techniques and keep a direct line to your team throughout. Any production risks are discussed before anything starts.

How often should we test?

At least once a year, and after any major change such as a new application, an infrastructure move or an acquisition.

What is the difference between a scan and a pen test?

A vulnerability scan is automated and lists potential weaknesses. A pen test adds skilled manual testing to confirm which ones can be exploited and what an attacker could reach.

Do you help us fix the issues?

Yes. Our report includes step-by-step remediation guidance, and our engineers can implement fixes or support your own team.
