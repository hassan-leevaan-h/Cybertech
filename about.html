// Our story

How It All Started

Our three founders who were studying IT grew frustrated at the lack of IT educational and learning opportunities in the Maldives and decided to do something about it. After finishing their respective degrees, they registered a company and started providing consultations to their friends and family from a small rental 2 room apartment. Fast forward to today, we are a team of engineers, testers, analysts and trainers who are capable of providing quality IT, cybersecurity and assurance service to a company of any scale. We also build software and make sense of data and invest our time and resources to train the next generation who will take up our cause.

Our approach is simple: understand the problem, make the fix sustainable and look for more opportunities to create value.
