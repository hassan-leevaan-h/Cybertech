// Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do we need a consultant if we already have an IT team?

Often yes. We complement your team with specialist experience and an independent view, particularly for audits, board reporting and one-off projects.

Can you help us get certified?

We prepare you for ISO 27001, SOC 2 and similar standards through gap analysis, policies and evidence collection. The certificate itself is issued by an independent auditor.

What does a retainer include?

A set number of advisory hours each month, quarterly risk reviews and priority access for urgent questions.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. Our workshops and assessments are scaled to your size, so you only pay for the depth you need.
