What we do.

Make your people your strongest defence.

Most incidents begin with a person, not a machine. Our training turns that risk into strength, with practical, up-to-date sessions delivered by working security professionals.

Choose from our standard courses or let us tailor a programme to your industry, tools and risks. Sessions run on-site, online or hybrid, for groups of any size.

// Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Can training be tailored to our company?

Yes. We adapt examples, labs and scenarios to your industry, systems and policies.

Do you offer online sessions?

Yes. Live online sessions include breakout labs and Q&A, and recordings are available for staff who miss a session.

How do we measure results?

We run pre- and post-training assessments and track phishing simulation results, then summarise progress in a report for management.

Do learners receive certificates?

Everyone who completes a course receives a certificate. Our bootcamps also prepare learners for industry exams.
